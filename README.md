One sentence, 189 characters, explaining the modern internet to a time traveler from 1800 — as a road from your desk to a library holding more letters and books than any king ever owned, where you may read any shelf and send your own words to any reader, quicker than a blink.

## The sentence

> Imagine a road from your desk to a library holding more letters and books than any king ever owned, where you may read any shelf and send your own words to any reader, quicker than a blink.

Every word in it is one a person from 1800 already knows — road, desk, library, letters, books, king, shelf, words, reader, blink — so the picture needs no word coined after the traveler's time.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). It is fully deterministic: one fixed string, standard library only, no network, no randomness.
